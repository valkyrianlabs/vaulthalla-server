- Discoverable passkey sign-in (#358): new ws commands auth.passkey.options (anonymous, empty allowCredentials, UV required, the same answer for every caller) and auth.passkey.login (userHandle required and equal to the credential owner's; completes through signin::complete at aal 2, amr hwk or swk; one uniform refusal). Additive under protocol 1; both are session-lifecycle commands, refused on signed-in (already_signed_in) and pending sockets, never reachable from the device listener.
- Discoverable sign-in challenges have their own store (at most 10,000 open, rate_limited when full); auth.passkey.options and auth.passkey.login are rate limited to 30 a minute per client address.
- Audit: auth.passkey.login (success {factor_id, amr}, failure).
- Console: Sign in with passkey on /login and conditional mediation (autofill) on the username field, loaded on demand; Playwright specs for discoverable sign-in, autofill and a synced passkey's non-growing counter (CDP setCredentialProperties).
