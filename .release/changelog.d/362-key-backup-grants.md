- Key-backup grants (#362, MFA design note 6.4, D1, D4): migration 130 `key_backup_grants` (one live per device; origin approved|migrated; approved_via; suspended_at; revoked_at/revoked_reason), `key_backup_grant_vaults` and the D1 snapshot `key_backup_grant_migration`; migration 129's interim `devices.key_backup_revoked_at/_reason` are carried into the snapshot and dropped (`Device` JSON loses `key_backup_revoked_at`).
- D1: at the first start after migration 130 every live device whose user holds encryption-key Export (or is a super admin) gets an origin `migrated` grant over exactly the vaults that user may export then (`keys.backup.grant.migrate` each); a device whose authority #360 had revoked gets it recorded revoked; nothing for anyone else.
- `vault.keys.export` (and `exportAll` under a device scope) needs no step-up only inside the device's live, unsuspended grant; otherwise the `keys` step-up bound to `vault.keys.export:<id>|all`. The interim `auth::stepup::keyBackupGrantCovers` is gone. Newly paired devices need `device.backup.grant.create` once.
- New commands `device.backup.grant.list|create|extend|confirm|revoke` (console and device sessions); create/extend/confirm annotated `device.trust` (targets `device.backup.grant.create:<row>:<ids>`, `.extend:<grant>:<ids>`, `.confirm:<grant>`), revoke none. New model `KeyBackupGrant`; refusal `recovery_cooldown` on create.
- Cap of 60 vault exports per grant per hour (in memory): then `keys.backup.grant.suspend` and an account notice (`keys.backup_grant_suspended`); confirm lifts it.
- Automatic revocation: with the device (`Devices::revoke|revokeAll`), the identities publication hook (deactivated, deleted, lost Export), recovery-code sign-in and MFA reset (`auth::mfa::recovery::revokeKeyBackupGrants`), devices idle 90 days (handshake, DB sweep, startup). Audit `keys.backup.grant.revoke {reason}` and `keys.backup.export {grant_id, device, versions}`.
- D4: during the recovery cooldown only the device's approval key registered before the recovery may approve re-creating the grant the recovery revoked, for a subset of its vaults (`auth::approval` lets exactly that device.trust target through the cooldown).
- Console: Devices › My devices "Key backup" column (confirm, turn off).
